ROSTERBASE → ROSTERLINK 2.0 BY Neonix Zone

*RosterBase is now RosterLink*  
RosterBase was originally created by `Radium and Koda`.  
Radium and koda have now joined the *Neonix Zone team*, and RosterBase has merged with Neonix Zone.
